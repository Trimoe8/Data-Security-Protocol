Late Thought Automation

Production tool for the YouTube channel “The Late Thought”: thoughts too big for a normal day.

Late Thought Automation is a private tool used only by the operator of the YouTube channel The Late Thought. It is not offered to other users.

What the tool does

  • Reads statistics of the operator's own channel (views, watch time, retention, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate) to plan better videos.
  • Uploads videos and thumbnails that the operator has reviewed and approved, as private videos with a scheduled publishing time.
  • Reads and updates titles and descriptions of the operator's own videos.
